About this tool

What it does

Category → Wikidata takes a category on any Wikimedia wiki – Wikipedia, Wikisource, Commons and the rest, in any language – and lists every page in it together with its Wikidata item. It can follow subcategories to a chosen depth, shows which category each page was found in, lists each page's parent categories, and checks the linked items for missing labels and descriptions in the wiki's own language. Pages without an item get a one-click link to create one. Results can be downloaded as CSV, TSV, JSON, a wikitext table or a plain list of QIDs, or opened in PetScan for larger queries.

The tool was created for the needs that came up in our ongoing projects: editors and campaign organisers kept asking which articles in a topic were connected to Wikidata, and which items still needed work. It is meant for edit-a-thons, photo campaigns, WikiProjects and anyone building or cleaning up a knowledge graph around a topic.

Wikimedia Biodiversity Taskforce

The Wikimedia Biodiversity Taskforce is a collective of volunteers dedicated to identifying and bridging knowledge gaps in biodiversity-related content across the Wikimedia ecosystem. Its projects include India's plant catalogue on Wikidata, Project Hortus Malabaricus, rare, endangered and threatened plants of Kerala, invasive alien species, sacred groves (kaavu), India biodiversity bibliography from the Biodiversity Heritage Library, Wiki Loves Birds and Wiki Loves Earth India, along with events such as Open Data Day and bioblitzes.

Learn more on the Taskforce's Meta-Wiki page.

Privacy and source

The tool does not store your queries beyond a short in-memory cache and loads no resources from outside Wikimedia. It reads only public data from the wiki you choose and from Wikidata. A JSON API is available for other tools.
